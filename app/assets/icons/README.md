# Monochrome launcher marks

Brand marks identify their respective services in CleanFrame. Their owners keep the trademark; the
marks are not an endorsement. The SVG paths are filled white and recoloured by the interface, which
also sets size and opacity. ABC uses a Lissajous mark from the MIT Custom Brand Icons source, SBS the
feather extracted from its MIT SBS Chill mark, and the labels next to them say which streaming
service is meant. Utility outlines (console, HDMI, PC, and so on) are original drawings on a 24-unit
grid.

Simple Icons sources are CC0 and Custom Brand Icons keeps its MIT licence; neither source record
grants trademark rights. See SOURCES.json for the exact revisions and adaptations.

Prime uses the two smile subpaths rather than clipped wordmark text, and Apple Music the note subpath
without the outer app tile. Both are deliberately simplified marks that sit beside the full service
name. The CC0 and MIT texts are included here, and the old Australian TV source's GPL text is kept
for provenance only, since no current icon uses that source.
